================================================================================
TRACK 03: PATH FINDER - OFFICIAL FAQ & CLARIFICATIONS
================================================================================

Thank you to all teams for reaching out. We have compiled your queries regarding 
Track 3 into this official FAQ. 

1. MISSING MATERIALS & DEGRADATION SCRIPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Will a Track 3 student package be provided?
A: Yes, the Track 3 Student Package is being distributed. Crucially, this 
includes the official degradation script (`degrade_imu.py`) which allows you to 
generate your own sample datasets with escalating noise, bias drift, dropouts, 
and spikes.

Q: What sample data should we use for development and testing?
A: You can freely use any sequence from the publicly available EuRoC MAV or 
TUM-VI datasets as your sample data. Simply apply the provided degradation script 
to these sequences to generate your own degraded testing sets.

2. INPUT DATA & PROBLEM CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Will waypoint positions or timestamps be supplied to the estimator?
A: No. Your pipeline must estimate the trajectory using ONLY the degraded IMU 
file. Waypoints are used internally by our evaluation pipeline to score your 
accuracy, but they are completely hidden from your algorithm.

Q: How is the 5-second blind segment handled? Do we append rows?
A: DO NOT append extra poses beyond the input timestamps. As per Slide 16, you 
must output *exactly one pose per IMU frame*. The evaluation dataset will 
contain timestamps during the blind 5-second window to tell you *when* to 
predict, but the sensor values will be unusable/invalid.

Q: How do 2-second dropouts appear in the CSV? 
A: As you will see in `degrade_imu.py`, dropouts are represented by physically 
MISSING rows. The script removes chunks of rows to simulate signal loss, meaning 
there will be sudden jumps in your timestamp delta.

3. EXECUTION LIMITS & IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Python vs C++? Is main.cpp accepted?
A: No. Although Slide 17 mentions `main.cpp`, we are requiring all teams to stick 
exclusively to Python for Track 3 to ensure a standardized automated evaluation 
environment. Please submit a standalone Python script (`main.py`).

Q: How is the 8 MB peak RAM limit measured (process vs algorithm)?
A: Because we require Python (where the interpreter alone exceeds 8 MB), the 8 MB 
constraint is measured at the ALGORITHM / HEAP level. We are profiling the memory 
footprint utilized by your data structures, buffers, and trajectory estimation 
logic, not the base OS-level RSS of the runtime.

Q: Processing order - Can we use future samples to refine earlier poses?
A: Yes. Slide 14 specifies that this is an "offline" task. You may read the 
entire CSV into memory (while strictly staying under the 8 MB limit) and perform 
non-causal / batch smoothing before generating your `estimated_trajectory.txt`.

4. SCORING & LOGISTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Will evo use alignment?
A: Yes, the estimated trajectory will be aligned to the hidden ground truth 
using SE(3) alignment (Umeyama) prior to computing the Absolute Trajectory Error 
(ATE). You do not need to guess the exact starting global coordinate frame.

Q: What is the required demo video length?
A: Please aim for 2 to 3 minutes, with a strict maximum of 3 minutes.

Q: What is the final deadline?
A: The final submission deadline is November 22, 2026. The submission Google 
Form link will be shared in the official announcements channel.
